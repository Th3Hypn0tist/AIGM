# USL – Universal Security Layer (legacy / non-normative stub)

USL is preserved as historical policy material from pre-3.0 AIGM.
It is NOT a normative AIGM 3.0 Core component and MUST NOT be interpreted as a required implementation layer.

Historical intent:
- user/content access validation and routing
- identity and permission handling
- audit-oriented controls

AIGM 3.0 normative authority and safety requirements are defined in the referenced standards under `standards/`, especially `aigm_authority.sh` and `aigm_safety.sh`.
