# HSL – Human Security Layer (legacy / non-normative stub)

HSL is preserved as historical policy material from pre-3.0 AIGM.
It is NOT a normative AIGM 3.0 Core component and MUST NOT be interpreted as a required implementation layer.

Historical intent:
- humane safeguards
- duty-of-care guidance
- incident and escalation practices

AIGM 3.0 normative safety requirements are defined in the referenced standards under `standards/`, especially `aigm_safety.sh`.
